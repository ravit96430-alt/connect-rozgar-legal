The short version

  • We keep your mobile number, name, gender, area, photo, skills and work history so the platform works.
  • Workers: an employer can reveal your phone number to call you about work, once you have agreed to this (§3). We tell you when it happens.
  • If you scan an Aadhaar card, the photo is read on your phone and is not uploaded, and we store only the last four digits of the number.

  • Photos are not password-protected: anyone who has a photo's link can open it (§3).
  • We do not sell your data, and we use no advertising or third-party analytics tools in the app.
  • You can delete your account in the app, or by email (§5). Deleting removes your photos as well.

4. Location, specifically

  • We store a worker's location coordinates (latitude and longitude) so jobs and workers can be matched, and a job's coordinates and place name so workers can travel there. Coordinates are also used to work out distances.
  • Before you accept a job, an employer sees only a rough distance band — "Under 2 km away", "2–5 km away", "5–10 km away" or "10+ km away" — not your exact position. A band is used because a precise figure asked from several points could reveal roughly where you are.
  • A job's place is shown to workers, because they must travel there. An employer's home address is shared only if they choose it as the work location.
  • Your area/address text and coordinates are removed when you delete your account (§5).

5. How long we keep it, and deleting your account

We keep your data while your account exists. We do not keep it for advertising or profiling.

Delete your account in the app: Profile → Delete Account. Or by email: write to ravit96430@gmail.com — see the Delete Account page for the steps, how we verify it is you, and timing.

How in-app deletion works (app version 1.0.1 and later). The server first removes the photos it can prove belong to your account, checks none remain, and only then removes the account itself. If any part of that fails, the app tells you your account was not deleted and lets you retry; it only says "deleted" after the server confirms it. Email requests are carried out through the same server process.

What is removed: your profile, your photos, your location, jobs you posted, your applications, messages you sent or received (they disappear for the other person too), ratings you gave or received, notifications, ID-verification status, push tokens, your terms-acceptance records, and your sign-in (including the stored mobile number).

What may remain, and why:

  • Payment and paid-contact-reveal records you took part in, with your name/identity link removed, for accounting and legal record-keeping. We keep these only for as long as we are required or entitled to for such records. We have not fixed an exact period and will state it here once settled.
  • Administrator activity records (for example, that an administrator deleted an account) which record the action, role and time and the account's internal ID number — no name, phone number or photo — kept for security and accountability.
  • A minimal deletion record (internal ID number, status, time and number of files removed) so that we can show a deletion was completed and stop new uploads from a deleted account's old sign-in.
  • Anti-abuse counters described in §1, which are not linked to your profile.
  • Your deletion request email and our reply, kept only as long as needed to handle the request and any follow-up.
  • Backups. Our hosting provider may keep backups for a limited time after deletion. We do not use them to bring back a deleted account and delete again if a backup is ever restored.

People and providers who already hold information — for example an employer who revealed your number, or a provider in §3 — keep it under their own rules; we cannot recall it.
