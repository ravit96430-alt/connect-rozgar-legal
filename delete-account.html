Option 1 — Delete in the app (fastest)

Open Connect Rozgar → Profile → Delete Account and confirm. This works from app version 1.0.1 (update the app first if you have an older version).

The server removes your photos, checks that none remain, and only then removes your account. If something goes wrong, the app tells you plainly that your account was not deleted and lets you retry. It shows "deleted" only after the server confirms.

Option 2 — Ask us by email

There is no web form on this page. Email ravit96430@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Connect Rozgar account" and tell us:

  • the mobile number your account uses, and your name as shown in the app;
  • optionally, your area and roughly when you joined, or a job you posted or applied to.

Never send us your password, a one-time code (OTP), or an image or full number of your Aadhaar or PAN. We will never ask for these.

How we check it is really you

We compare what you tell us with the account (registered mobile number, name and a detail or two of your activity). If we need more certainty, we may ask you to sign in to the app and change your profile name to a short word we email to you, which shows you control the account — no password or OTP is ever needed. If you cannot sign in at all, we will talk with you about other non-secret details that can identify the account. We do not delete an account until we are reasonably sure the request is from its owner, because deleting someone else's account by mistake cannot be undone.

How long it takes (our service targets)

  • We aim to acknowledge your email within 2 working days.
  • We aim to complete deletion within 7 calendar days after your ownership is verified.

These are targets we work to, not legal guarantees. We will email you when it is done.

What deletion removes

  • Your profile, photos (profile and job photos), location, and sign-in (including the stored mobile number).
  • Jobs you posted, your applications, messages you sent or received (they also disappear for the other person), ratings you gave or received, notifications, ID-verification status, push tokens and terms-acceptance records.

It cannot be undone. Signing up again later starts a brand new account.

What may be kept, and why: payment and paid-contact-reveal records you were part of (with your name and identity link removed, for accounting and legal record-keeping — for as long as required for such records; we have not fixed an exact period); a minimal deletion record and administrator activity record containing only an internal ID number, action, role and time (no name, phone number or photo); anti-abuse counters not tied to your profile; your deletion-request email and our reply, for as long as needed to handle the request; and provider backups, which may exist for a limited time. An employer who already saw your number keeps it — we cannot take it back. Full details are in the Privacy Policy (§5).
